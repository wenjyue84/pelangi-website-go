# Data Breach Incident Response Procedure

**Pelangi Capsule Hostel**
**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Authority:** Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024

---

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the step-by-step procedure for responding to a personal data breach in compliance with Malaysia's PDPA 2024 Amendment mandatory breach notification requirements.

## 2. Scope

Applies to any unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal, or similar action involving personal data processed by Pelangi Capsule Hostel — whether digital or physical.

## 4. Breach Response Timeline

| Step | Deadline | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Detection** | T+0 | Breach is identified or reported |
| **Initial Assessment** | T+4 hours | DPO assesses severity, scope, and data involved |
| **Containment** | T+12 hours | Stop ongoing breach, preserve evidence |
| **Commissioner Notification** | **T+72 hours** | Notify PDP Commissioner (JPDP) |
| **Data Subject Notification** | **T+7 days** (from Commissioner notification) | Notify affected individuals |
| **Post-Incident Review** | T+30 days | Root cause analysis and preventive measures |

## 5. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Detection and Initial Report

- Any staff member who suspects a data breach must immediately report it to the DPO at **dpo@pelangicapsulehostel.com** or **+60 12-708 8789**.
- Record:
  - Date and time the breach was discovered
  - How the breach was discovered
  - Who discovered it
  - Initial description of what happened

### Step 2: Initial Assessment (within 4 hours)

The DPO must assess:

- **What data was affected?** (names, passport numbers, contact details, payment info, etc.)
- **How many individuals are affected?**
- **What was the cause?** (hacking, accidental disclosure, lost device, physical theft, etc.)
- **Is the breach ongoing?** (can it be stopped immediately?)
- **What is the risk of harm to affected individuals?**

### Step 3: Containment (within 12 hours)

- Isolate affected systems (change passwords, revoke access, take systems offline if needed)
- Preserve evidence (logs, screenshots, access records)
- If physical records: secure the area, recover documents if possible
- Do NOT delete evidence or modify logs

### Step 4: Commissioner Notification (within 72 hours)

**Mandatory under PDPA 2024 Amendment.**

Notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner (JPDP):

- **Online:** Submit via the JPDP portal at [www.pdp.gov.my](https://www.pdp.gov.my)
- **Email:** aduan@pdp.gov.my (if portal unavailable)

The notification must include:

1. Description of the nature of the breach
2. Categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
3. Categories and approximate number of personal data records affected
4. Name and contact details of the DPO
5. Description of likely consequences of the breach
6. Description of measures taken or proposed to address the breach

### Step 5: Data Subject Notification (within 7 days of Commissioner notification)

**Mandatory under PDPA 2024 Amendment.**

Notify affected individuals via email (or phone if email unavailable):

**Template:**

> Subject: Important Notice: Data Breach Affecting Your Personal Information
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> We are writing to inform you of a personal data breach that may affect your personal information held by Pelangi Capsule Hostel.
>
> **What happened:** [Brief description]
>
> **What data was involved:** [Types of data affected]
>
> **When it happened:** [Date/time of breach]
>
> **What we are doing:** [Steps taken to contain and remediate]
>
> **What you can do:** [Recommended protective actions — e.g., change passwords, monitor accounts]
>
> **Contact:** If you have questions, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@pelangicapsulehostel.com or +60 12-708 8789.
>
> We have notified the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as required by law.
>
> We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience.

### Step 6: Post-Incident Review (within 30 days)

- Conduct root cause analysis
- Document lessons learned
- Implement preventive measures
- Update security procedures if needed
- File the incident report for record-keeping (retain for minimum 7 years)

## 6. Record-Keeping

Maintain a breach register with the following for each incident:

- Date of breach and date of discovery
- Description and cause
- Data and individuals affected
- Actions taken (containment, notification)
- Commissioner notification date and reference
- Data subject notification date
- Post-incident review findings

## 7. Penalties for Non-Compliance

Under the PDPA 2024 Amendment:

- Failure to notify the Commissioner within 72 hours: fine up to **RM250,000** and/or imprisonment up to **2 years**
- Failure to notify data subjects within 7 days: fine up to **RM250,000** and/or imprisonment up to **2 years**

## 8. Review

This procedure shall be reviewed annually by the DPO and updated as required to reflect changes in law or operational practices.
