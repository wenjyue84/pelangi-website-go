Getting to JB from India

Chennai → JB

AirAsia and IndiGo fly Chennai (MAA) to Kuala Lumpur (KUL). From KUL TBS terminal, take a direct JB bus (4h, RM25–35). Or fly Chennai to Senai Airport (JHB) if direct flights are available on your dates.

Bangalore / Hyderabad → JB

Fly to KUL via AirAsia, then take the bus from TBS to JB Sentral (Larkin) or JBCC. Grab from the bus terminal to Pelangi Capsule Hostel is RM8–12.

Mumbai / Delhi → JB

Multiple carriers (Air India, AirAsia, Malaysia Airlines) connect to KUL in 5–6h. From KUL, bus or domestic flight to JB. Grab within JB is under RM15 from most entry points.

Via Singapore (common route for SG-based Indians)

Cross on foot or by bus through the Woodlands CIQ checkpoint: take the Causeway Link CW1 bus from Queen St Terminal or Woodlands Bus Interchange to JB Sentral. From JB Sentral, Grab to Taman Pelangi takes 10–15 minutes and costs RM8–12. If you arrive at Larkin bus terminal (JB's main long-distance hub) instead, a Grab to the hostel is a similar short ride.

Tip: Book a Grab from Larkin Sentral terminal, Senai Airport, or JBCC (City Square) directly to Taman Pelangi. The hostel address is 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi, 80400 Johor Bahru.

Visa Information for Indian Passport Holders

eNTRI (eNotification of Travel Information) — 15-day stay, apply online before travel. No visa sticker required. Suitable for short tourism trips.
eVisa — For longer stays or specific purposes. Apply at Malaysia's official immigration portal.
Singapore Pass holders — Indian nationals on a valid Singapore Employment Pass (EP), S Pass, or Long-Term Pass entering Malaysia from Singapore may enter visa-free for short stays. Confirm current rules at imi.gov.my before travel.

Frequently Asked Questions — Indian Travelers

Do I need a visa as an Indian national to enter Malaysia?
Indian passport holders can apply for an eNTRI (15 days, online) or eVisa at Malaysia's official immigration portal before arrival. Indian nationals on a valid Singapore EP/S Pass entering from Singapore may enter Malaysia visa-free for short stays — always verify the current rules at imi.gov.my.
Is vegetarian food available near the hostel?
Yes. Mamak stalls within 5 minutes' walk serve roti canai, dhal, dosai, and vegetarian nasi campur daily (including 24 hours at some). South Indian banana leaf restaurants are in Jalan Dhoby (Little India), a short Grab ride away.
How do I get to Pelangi from Senai International Airport?
Take a Grab or airport taxi to 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi, Johor Bahru. The ride is 20–30 minutes and costs approximately RM25–35. No direct public bus connects Senai to Taman Pelangi.
Can I pay in Indian Rupees or use GPay?
We accept Malaysian Ringgit cash, DuitNow QR (GrabPay, Touch 'n Go, major e-wallets), and credit/debit cards. GPay is not directly accepted, but money changers at JB CIQ, City Square mall, and Larkin terminal offer good rates for INR → MYR. Indian Visa/Mastercard debit cards work at most ATMs.
Is the hostel safe for solo Indian travelers?
Yes. Pelangi has 24/7 CCTV, keypad-secured entry, and personal lockers inside each pod room. Each capsule pod has a sliding door that locks from inside. Taman Pelangi is a safe, family-friendly neighbourhood with plenty of eateries and regular foot traffic at all hours.
What is the current MYR to INR exchange rate?
The Ringgit-to-Rupee rate moves daily, so we quote our rates in RM only and don't publish a fixed INR figure. Check a live converter (Google, XE) right before booking to see today's exact amount for RM35/night, RM30 for a new guest's first night, or RM600/month.
