Crossing the Border from Singapore

Two land crossings connect Singapore and Johor Bahru

Causeway (Woodlands)

Most popular — 24 hours

  • Bus CW1: JB Sentral ↔ Kranji MRT — RM 2.50 / SGD 1
  • Bus 170: Larkin → Queen St, Singapore
  • Grab/Taxi: RM 40–60 one-way to SG city
  • Peak hours: 7–9 AM and 5–8 PM weekdays
  • Weekend peak: Fri evening & Sun evening

Second Link (Tuas)

Less crowded, faster clearance

  • Bus CW2: JB Sentral → Tuas — RM 3.50
  • Best for: Private car or during Causeway jams
  • Nearest MRT: Tuas Link (East-West line)
  • Grab: ~30% cheaper than Causeway route at peak
  • Travel time: 20–40 min depending on traffic
Immigration tip: Have your passport ready before reaching the counter. Malaysia exit and Singapore entry are separate checkpoints — stay on the bus between them. Off-peak, the whole crossing takes 30–45 minutes.

MyNIISe QR Immigration

Faster border clearance with Malaysia’s digital immigration system

How to set up MyNIISe QR (step-by-step)

  1. 1

    Download the MyNIISe app

    Available on iOS App Store and Google Play Store. Search for “MyNIISe” by the Immigration Department of Malaysia.

  2. 2

    Register your account

    Create an account using your passport number, nationality, and a valid email address. Malaysian citizens can use MyKad.

  3. 3

    Complete your traveller profile

    Enter your passport details, upload a passport photo, and fill in the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) information.

  4. 4

    Generate your QR code

    Before travelling, open the app and generate your entry/exit QR code. This replaces the paper arrival card.

  5. 5

    Scan at the border

    At JB CIQ, use the dedicated MyNIISe QR lanes (marked with signs). Scan your QR code at the e-gate — no more queuing at manual counters.

Benefits

  • ✓ Skip the manual counter queue
  • ✓ No paper arrival card to fill
  • ✓ Works for all nationalities
  • ✓ Free to use

Good to know

  • • Set up before you travel (needs internet)
  • • Keep your passport handy — still required
  • • QR lanes may have shorter wait times
  • • App works offline once QR is generated

DuitNow–PayNow Cross-Border Payments

Pay in Malaysia directly from your Singapore bank account

How it works

  • 1. Open your Singapore banking app (DBS PayLah!, OCBC, UOB, etc.)
  • 2. Scan the Malaysian merchant’s DuitNow QR code
  • 3. Amount converts automatically at bank rate (SGD → MYR)
  • 4. Payment is instant — no cash or money changer needed

Where you can use it

  • ✓ Most restaurants and cafes displaying DuitNow QR
  • ✓ Convenience stores (7-Eleven, KK Mart)
  • ✓ Shopping malls and retail shops
  • ✓ Some Grab rides and food delivery
  • ✓ Pelangi Capsule Hostel — we accept DuitNow QR
Singaporean guests: DuitNow–PayNow QR interoperability means you can pay at most Malaysian shops without exchanging cash. The exchange rate is your bank’s live rate — usually better than airport money changers.
