Tourists in Distress: Pelangi Helps

Something went wrong on your trip? You are not alone. Here is how to get help in Johor Bahru, and what Pelangi Capsule Hostel can do for you.

In an emergency, call 999 first

Malaysia's emergency number is 999 (police, ambulance, fire). Dialling 112 from a mobile phone is redirected to 999. If anyone is hurt, get medical help before anything else. Everything below can wait until people are safe.

24-Hour Free Stay After a Road Accident

As part of our community responsibility (CSR), Pelangi may let a tourist who has been in a road accident stay free for 24 hours. The idea is simple: you get a safe place to rest and time to contact your family and arrange payment for any stay afterwards.

What you need to bring

  • ✓ A police report about the accident
  • ✓ Evidence of the accident (for example photos or documents)
  • ✓ Your passport or ID, as for any hostel check-in

Fair limits

  • ✓ Subject to availability on the day
  • ✓ Subject to management discretion
  • ✓ The free period is 24 hours, to give you time to contact family and arrange payment for any stay afterwards

The Police Station Is Next Door

There is a police station just beside our capsules in Taman Pelangi. After an accident, a theft or a lost passport, we encourage you to go there and make a police report. A police report is often requested by insurers and embassies, so confirm with them what they need.

Our address: 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi, 80400 Johor Bahru.

How to Ask for Help

  1. Get to safety and get medical help if needed (999).
  2. Make a police report at the station next to Pelangi.
  3. Keep your evidence: photos, documents, the report.
  4. Message us on WhatsApp and tell us briefly what happened.
  5. Come to the hostel with your report and evidence.

WhatsApp Pelangi: +60 10-308 4289

Overstayed and Want to Go Home?

If your permitted stay has run out and you want to return to your home country, we can guide you through the SPLP (Special Pass) process step by step. The pass and any compound are handled by Malaysian Immigration, so always confirm the current requirements with them directly. Start with the overstay guide above, then message us if you get stuck.

Do you have 5,000 or more followers and want to make a video about Pelangi? You can get a free stay. The number of nights depends on the quality of your video and the size of your following, and is negotiable. See the creators page or message us on WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stay free at Pelangi after a road accident?

Possibly. With a police report and accident evidence, we may offer up to 24 hours free so you can contact family and arrange payment. It depends on availability and management discretion.

How do I ask for help?

Message us on WhatsApp at +60 10-308 4289, tell us what happened and bring your police report and evidence.

Is the free stay guaranteed?

No. It is a goodwill gesture, subject to availability and management discretion.

This page is general information, not legal or medical advice. Rules and procedures can change, so confirm with the relevant authority (police, hospital, embassy or Immigration).
