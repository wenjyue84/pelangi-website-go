Emergency & Help for Tourists in Johor Bahru

Illness, a road accident, a lost passport or an overstay can happen to anyone. Start here for calm, practical guides, and see what Pelangi Capsule Hostel can do to help.

In an emergency, call 999 first

Malaysia's emergency number is 999. Dialling 112 from a mobile phone also reaches emergency services. Get medical help before anything else; everything in the guides below can wait until people are safe.
