High-Speed WiFi

Gigabit fiber internet connection with 50+ Mbps download speeds. Perfect for streaming, video calls, and remote work. Coverage throughout the hostel.

Quiet Hours

10 PM - 8 AM quiet hours policy ensures peaceful sleep. Common areas are available 24/7 for guests who need to work around the clock.

Secure Lockers

Individual lockers in each pod with master key access. Valuables storage available. 24/7 access to your personal locker.

Universal Power Outlets

Multiple 220V outlets and USB charging ports in each pod. Lightning, USB-C, and Micro USB adapters available at reception.

Co-working Space

Dedicated co-working area with high-speed WiFi, desk, and ergonomic seating. Free to use for all guests. Bookable for business meetings. Conference call-friendly.

Full Air Conditioning

Climate-controlled pods with individual temperature controls. Central air conditioning maintains 22-24°C comfort year-round.

Fresh Linens Daily

Complimentary fresh sheets, pillowcases, and towels. Laundry service available daily. Duvet covers changed weekly or on request.

Shared Kitchen

Fully equipped kitchen with refrigerator, microwave, stove, oven, and dishwasher. Open 6 AM - 11 PM. Complimentary cooking utensils and basic spices provided.

Laundry Facilities

Complimentary washing machine and dryer (RM3/load). In-house laundry service for guests: RM5/load (since March 2026) — cheaper than the dobi outside. Iron and ironing board available.

Our Sustainability Commitment

At Pelangi Capsule Hostel, we're dedicated to minimizing our environmental impact while providing exceptional hospitality. Sustainable travel isn't just a trend — it's our responsibility to future generations of travelers exploring Johor Bahru.

Water Dispensers

Complimentary refillable water dispensers throughout the hostel reduce single-use plastic bottles. We provide reusable water bottles to guests to further minimize waste.

Energy-Efficient Lighting

All capsule pods and common areas are equipped with LED lighting and motion-sensor lighting in restrooms and corridors. This reduces electricity consumption while maintaining comfort.

Comprehensive Recycling

Color-coded recycling bins for paper, plastic, glass, and organic waste are available in all common areas and throughout the hostel. Staff ensure proper sorting and disposal with local recycling facilities.

Eco-Conscious Operations

We source local and sustainable products whenever possible, use biodegradable cleaning supplies, and prioritize eco-friendly amenities. Our commitment to sustainability is integral to how we operate daily.

By choosing Pelangi Capsule Hostel, you're supporting sustainable tourism in Johor Bahru. Together, we can make travel more responsible and protect the beautiful destinations we all love to explore.
