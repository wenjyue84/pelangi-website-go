Pelangi Capsule Hostel

Japanese-style Capsule Accommodation · Johor Bahru

Pelangi Capsule Hostel is a Japanese-style capsule accommodation hostel located in Taman Pelangi, Johor Bahru, Malaysia. Founded in 2023, the hostel was designed to bring modern, affordable capsule accommodation to budget travellers and backpackers visiting the region. Every detail — from the Japanese-inspired capsule layout to the 24/7 self-check-in system — was created with guest comfort and accessibility in mind.

The hostel blog features first-hand guides to Johor Bahru and the Singapore–JB corridor: crossing the Causeway, eating well on a backpacker budget, day-tripping into Singapore, and getting around by ETS train, bus, and Grab. The advice comes from years of hosting guests who make the crossing and from deep knowledge of the border region.

What we offer

  • Budget and capsule accommodation in Johor Bahru (RM35/night, or RM30 first night for direct website bookings)
  • Japanese-style capsule pods with climate control and personal lighting
  • 24/7 self-check-in and access, no curfew
  • Free high-speed WiFi, shared kitchen, laundry service
  • 15 minutes from Singapore (Woodlands CIQ checkpoint)

About the hostel

  • Founded in 2023 in Taman Pelangi, Johor Bahru
  • 20 modern capsule pods with individual climate control
  • Designed for budget travellers, backpackers, and digital nomads
  • Hosts thousands of guests annually from across the region
