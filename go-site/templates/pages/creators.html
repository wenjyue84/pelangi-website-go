Content Creators Stay Free

Make an honest video about Pelangi Capsule Hostel and our neighbourhood in Johor Bahru, and stay with us for free.

Who this is for

YouTubers with 5,000 or more followers. This is a management offer: nights are agreed case by case and subject to availability.

What we ask

  • ✓ A video about Pelangi Capsule Hostel
  • ✓ Show the neighbourhood around Taman Pelangi, Johor Bahru
  • ✓ Clear disclosure that your stay was free (see below)

What we offer

  • ✓ Free nights at the hostel
  • ✓ The number of nights depends on the quality of your video and the number of your followers
  • ✓ Negotiable: tell us what you have in mind

How to apply

  1. Message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Send a link to your channel and tell us roughly when you would like to stay.
  3. Tell us the kind of video you plan to make about Pelangi and the neighbourhood.
  4. We reply with what we can offer. Nights are agreed together.

WhatsApp us: +60 10-308 4289

Please disclose the free stay

A free stay in return for a video is a commercial relationship, even when no money changes hands. Good practice, and what we ask of you:

  • ✓ Say it in the video, early, not only at the end or hidden behind a "show more" link.
  • ✓ Put a plain label such as #ad or "sponsored" with the video where viewers will see it, for example "Thanks to Pelangi Capsule Hostel for the free stay".
  • ✓ Use YouTube's "paid promotion" setting in the video details.

Malaysia's Content Code (administered by the Content Forum under MCMC) expects endorsements made in exchange for cash or another reciprocal arrangement to be disclosed as advertisements. The US FTC gives similar guidance. Rules differ by country, so check the ones that apply to you and your audience. This is general information, not legal advice.

What we do not do

  • ✗ We do not ask for scripted reviews.
  • ✗ We do not approve or edit your content.
  • ✗ We do not ask you to say only good things. Be honest with your viewers.

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply?

YouTubers with 5,000 or more followers who will make a video about Pelangi and the neighbourhood.

How many free nights?

It depends on the quality of the video and your follower count, and it is negotiable. There is no fixed table.

Is every request accepted?

Not automatically. Stays are subject to availability and management discretion.
