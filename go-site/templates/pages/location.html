Getting Here

Transport Directions

JB Sentral (Train Station)

~10 min drive / 5 km

  1. 1 Exit JB Sentral main entrance and head to the taxi/e-hailing pickup area
  2. 2 Take a Grab or taxi to 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi (RM8–12)
  3. 3 Alternatively, take bus P101 from JB Sentral bus stop to Pelangi Leisure Mall (15 min), then walk 2 minutes

CIQ (Customs, Immigration & Quarantine)

~15 min drive / 6 km

  1. 1 After clearing immigration, exit through the main hall towards the taxi stand
  2. 2 Take a Grab or taxi to 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi (RM10–15)
  3. 3 If arriving from Singapore by bus (CW1/CW2), alight at JB Sentral and follow JB Sentral directions above

Larkin Bus Terminal

~15 min drive / 7 km

  1. 1 Exit the terminal and head to the Grab/taxi pickup area
  2. 2 Take a Grab or taxi to 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi (RM10–15)
  3. 3 Alternatively, take bus P101 heading towards JB Sentral — alight at Pelangi Leisure Mall stop

Senai International Airport (JHB)

~35 min drive / 30 km

  1. 1 Take a Grab or airport taxi from the arrivals hall (RM45–60)
  2. 2 Alternatively, take the airport shuttle to JB Sentral, then Grab to Taman Pelangi
  3. 3 Direct Grab is the most convenient option for late-night arrivals
