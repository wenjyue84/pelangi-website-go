Getting to JB: Transport Options

  • Causeway Link CW1 bus — RM2.70 one-way from Woodlands Bus Interchange (Singapore) to JB CIQ. Most convenient for foot passengers. Return ticket available at JB CIQ terminal.
  • Grab from JB CIQ — after clearing Malaysian immigration, Grab to Taman Pelangi takes 10–15 min (~RM8–12). Book via the Grab app using your Singapore number.
  • Personal vehicle or cross-border Grab — toll RM2.90 (Malaysia side). Consider Tuas Second Link if the Causeway is gridlocked — typically less congested.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do a same-day visa run from Singapore to JB?

Yes — cross into Malaysia, wait a few hours, and return. Immigration accepts this. However, queue times are unpredictable: a same-day run can easily consume 4–6 hours. Overnight is less stressful and costs only ~SGD10 more.

What bus goes from Woodlands Bus Interchange to JB CIQ?

Causeway Link CW1. One-way fare RM2.70 (~SGD0.80). It departs frequently from Woodlands Bus Interchange (5 min walk from Woodlands MRT) and goes directly to JB CIQ terminal.

When are the Causeway queues worst?

Monday to Friday 7–9 AM and 5–8 PM are peak hours. Friday evenings and public holiday eves are the worst. The best window is Tuesday to Thursday, 10 AM–2 PM. The Tuas Second Link is usually less congested than the Woodlands Causeway.

Can EP or S Pass holders do a visa run to JB?

Yes. EP/S Pass holders have long-term Singapore work permits and are not subject to Singapore's tourist entry limits. However, if their Malaysia Social Visit Pass is expiring, they still need to exit and re-enter Malaysia to reset it — exactly what a JB visa run does.

Do I need to bring proof of accommodation?

Not strictly mandatory, but strongly recommended. Malaysian immigration may ask where you are staying. A Pelangi Capsule Hostel booking confirmation on your phone removes any uncertainty and speeds up the process.
