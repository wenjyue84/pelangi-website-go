Frequently Asked Questions

Singapore offers Employment Pass (EP) for professionals earning ≥SGD 5,000/month, S Pass for mid-skilled workers earning ≥SGD 3,150/month, and Work Permit (WP) for semi-skilled workers in construction, manufacturing, marine, and services.

EP typically requires a recognised degree. S Pass requires a diploma or technical certificate. Work Permits have no formal education requirement but your employer must meet a foreign worker quota.

Salaries vary: SGD 2,000–3,500 for WP holders, SGD 3,150–5,000 for S Pass, and SGD 5,000–15,000+ for EP holders. Overtime and shift allowances are common.

Yes. All Singapore work passes must be applied for by a registered employer or employment agent. You cannot self-sponsor.

EP applications take 3–8 weeks via the EP eService. S Pass and WP applications usually take 1–3 weeks.
