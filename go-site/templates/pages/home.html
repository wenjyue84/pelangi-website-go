About Pelangi Capsule Hostel

Is Pelangi Capsule Hostel near CIQ?

Yes, Pelangi Capsule Hostel is approximately 15 minutes by car from the Sultan Iskandar Building (CIQ) border checkpoint in Johor Bahru. The hostel is located at 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi, 80400 Johor Bahru — a central neighbourhood known for its night market, restaurants, and cafes. A Grab ride from JB CIQ to the hostel costs around RM8–12, making it one of the most accessible budget accommodations for travellers crossing from Singapore.

How much does a capsule pod cost?

Capsule pods at Pelangi Capsule Hostel start from RM30 per night on weekdays and RM35 per night on weekends (approximately SGD 9–10.50 or USD 6.50–7.50). Monthly long-stay rates are available from RM594 per month. Booking directly at pelangicapsulehostel.com/book guarantees the best rate with no OTA commissions or hidden fees.

Why is Pelangi Capsule one of the best hostels in Johor Bahru?

Pelangi Capsule Hostel combines Japanese-style capsule pod privacy with Malaysian hospitality at budget-friendly prices. Each pod has a lockable sliding door, personal climate control, USB charging, adjustable LED lighting, and a premium mattress. The hostel provides free high-speed WiFi, 24/7 self-check-in access, hot showers, a shared kitchen, personal lockers, and laundry facilities. Located in Taman Pelangi — one of JB's most vibrant food districts — guests are within walking distance of hawker stalls, kopitiam, and the famous Tuesday night market.
