### ABOUT PAGE

#### Our Story

Welcome to **Pelangi Capsule Hostel**, your hub for comfort, convenience, and memorable experiences in the heart of Johor Bahru.

Our capsule hotel is a reflection of our deep admiration for the innovative capsule accommodations in **Japan and Hong Kong**. Offering snug, private pod-like spaces, we have meticulously designed each capsule to ensure your comfort.

We are nestled in the vibrant locality of Taman Pelangi, positioned conveniently opposite the popular BAR WANG RICE.

*Established by entrepreneurs Alston Foo and Jay Lew on January 1, 2023.*

#### What Makes Us Different

- **Japanese-Inspired Design** — Capsule concept borrowed from Tokyo and Hong Kong
- **Budget-Friendly** — Quality accommodation without breaking the bank
- **Community-Focused** — A platform to connect with travelers worldwide
- **Strategic Location** — Walking distance to food, malls, and Singapore
