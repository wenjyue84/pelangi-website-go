## 1. Full Introduction (English)

### Overview
**Pelangi Capsule Hostel** (彩虹胶囊旅舍) is a Japanese-style capsule hostel in the
heart of **Taman Pelangi, Johor Bahru, Malaysia**. It offers travellers the privacy
of an enclosed, lockable sleeping pod at backpacker prices — a modern, clean, and
secure base just **15 minutes from Singapore**. Established in late 2022 and
owner-operated since, it has grown into a profitable, well-reviewed budget-stay brand
on the Singapore–JB corridor.

- **Address:** 26A Jalan Perang, Taman Pelangi, 80400 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia
- **Website:** https://pelangicapsulehostel.com · **Wikidata:** Q140363023
- **Coordinates:** 1.48552, 103.75940 · **Maps:** https://maps.app.goo.gl/maLXetUqYS5MtSLD9

### The concept
Inspired by the efficient capsule hotels of **Japan and Hong Kong**, each guest gets a
private, enclosed pod rather than an open dormitory bed — a sliding door that **locks
from the inside**, your own light and climate control, and a personal locker. It's the
privacy of a small hotel room at the price of a hostel bunk: ideal for solo travellers,
backpackers, digital nomads, and Singapore day-trippers.

### Accommodation
24 capsule pods across two types (single occupancy each):

| Pod type | Position | Rate | Long-stay |
|----------|----------|------|-----------|
| **Lower Deck Pod** | Ground level — no ladder, easy access | **RM45/night** | **RM594/month** |
| **Upper Deck Pod** | Elevated bunk — more privacy, less foot traffic | **RM35/night** | — |

*A Malaysia Tourism Tax of RM10/room/night applies to foreign tourists only.*

**Every pod includes:** lockable sliding privacy door · personal climate control (fan +
air-conditioning) · adjustable LED reading light · USB + power outlets · premium
mattress · personal locker · fresh towel.

### Shared facilities
Free high-speed WiFi throughout (good enough for video calls / remote work) · hot
showers with body wash & shampoo · fully equipped shared kitchen · water dispenser ·
in-house laundry (RM5/load) · luggage storage (before check-in / after check-out) ·
common lounge for socialising and working · **24/7 CCTV and secure keypad entry**.

### Practical info
- **Check-in:** from 3:00 PM, **self check-in available 24/7** (no curfew, no lockout)
- **Check-out:** before 12:00 PM
- **Payment:** Cash (MYR) · DuitNow QR · Credit card
- **Languages:** English · Chinese · Malay

### Location & getting here
Taman Pelangi is one of JB's most vibrant, food-rich neighbourhoods:
- **15 minutes** by car from Singapore's **Woodlands CIQ** checkpoint; short ride from **JB Sentral / CIQ**
- Short Grab ride from **Senai International Airport**
- The **Taman Pelangi Night Market (pasar malam)** runs every **Tuesday from 4:30 PM right at the doorstep**
- Walking distance to local eateries (San Low Seafood, Restoran Hua Mui); near **KOMTAR JBCC**, JB Old Chinese Temple, the Glass Temple, and the JB Waterfront; **Legoland Malaysia ~25 min** away

### Who it's for
Backpackers and budget travellers · solo and female travellers wanting privacy +
security · **digital nomads / remote workers** (monthly long-stay from RM594) ·
Singapore residents using JB as an affordable base for day trips · travellers transiting
via Senai or Changi airports.

### The story & team
Built in **2022–2023** by co-founder and owner **Jay Lew** after years of backpacking
across Japan and Southeast Asia — he designed the capsule layout and the 24/7
self-check-in system himself, to give travellers the privacy and freedom he wanted on
the road. Co-founder **Alston** runs day-to-day operations and guest relations.
Business milestones from internal records: SSM business registration (Sep 2022),
tenancy secured (Jan 2023), ~RM35k profit in its first full year (2023), an in-house
**AI WhatsApp assistant** launched (Dec 2023), and **expansion to a second capsule
unit** underway (deposit placed Oct 2025).
