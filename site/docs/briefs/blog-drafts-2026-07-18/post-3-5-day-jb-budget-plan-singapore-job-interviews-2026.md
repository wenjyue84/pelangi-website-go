<h2>Transport: Getting to Singapore for Your Interview</h2>

<p>This is the part people worry about most. Here's the exact process, timed for a 9:00 AM or 10:00 AM Singapore interview start.</p>

<h3>Option A: Causeway Link CW1 or SBS 170 Express Bus (Cheapest)</h3>

<ol>
  <li>Grab from Pelangi to JB CIQ (about 10 minutes, RM8–12 depending on time)</li>
  <li>Board Causeway Link CW1 or SBS 170 at JB CIQ — fare RM5.50 or SGD1.50 (buy a Singapore EZ-Link card if you don't have one; use it for onward MRT too)</li>
  <li>Clear Malaysian exit immigration at JB CIQ</li>
  <li>Cross the Causeway (the bus stops and everyone walks through Singapore customs at Woodlands Checkpoint)</li>
  <li>Arrive at Woodlands Interchange — connect to MRT (North-South Line) toward the city</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Total crossing time:</strong> 45–75 minutes on a weekday morning if you leave before 7:30 AM. After 8:00 AM the queues build and you're looking at 90+ minutes.</p>

<p><strong>Departure from Pelangi for a 9:00 AM Singapore interview:</strong> Leave by 7:00 AM. This sounds early but it leaves buffer for queue variance and avoids the peak crush.</p>

<h3>Option B: Grab Cross-Border (More Comfortable)</h3>

<p>Book a cross-border Grab from JB to Singapore. The driver handles the routing; you still exit the car at both immigration checkpoints and re-board. Cost: RM45–80 depending on surge pricing and time of day. Faster than the bus in the sense that you don't wait for departure times, but not significantly faster if border queues are long.</p>

<p>Recommended for interview days when you want zero logistical stress and you're willing to pay for it.</p>

<h3>Option C: RTS Link (From January 2027)</h3>

<p>If your interviews extend into early 2027, the RTS Link from Bukit Chagar to Woodlands North will be operational. A Grab from Pelangi to Bukit Chagar takes about 10 minutes. The train crossing is 5 minutes. Onward by Thomson-East Coast Line MRT. Expected fare: RM15.50–RM21.70 one way. Significantly faster than any current option at peak hours — and much more predictable.</p>

<h2>Visa-Run Angles for Job Seekers</h2>

<p>If you're a foreigner on a Malaysia Social Visit Pass doing rounds of Singapore interviews, JB is a natural base because it also solves your visa-run problem. The Malaysia SVP grants 30–90 days depending on nationality. When it expires, you can cross to Singapore (which resets the SVP on return), or take a longer visa run to Thailand, Batam, or Brunei from JB.</p>

<p>For Singapore residents who are in between jobs and want to stay close to Singapore during their job search without paying Singapore accommodation prices, the same logic applies — JB puts you 45–90 minutes from any Singapore interview, at a fraction of the cost.</p>
