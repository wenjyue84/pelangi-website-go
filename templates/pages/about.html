Our Story

A New Way to Stay
in Johor Bahru

Inspired by the efficient capsule hotels of Japan and Hong Kong, Pelangi Capsule Hostel brings this innovative accommodation concept to Johor Bahru.

Founded in 2023, we offer travelers and budget-conscious guests a unique lodging experience that combines privacy, comfort, and affordability in one modern package.

Each capsule is your private sanctuary — equipped with climate control, adjustable lighting, and power outlets — giving you everything you need for a restful stay.
