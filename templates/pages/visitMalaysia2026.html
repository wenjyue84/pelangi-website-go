Top JB Attractions

Must-visit spots in Johor Bahru for Visit Malaysia 2026 — all easily reachable from Pelangi Capsule Hostel in Taman Pelangi.

Johor Bahru City Square

Shopping & Transit

Major shopping mall connected to JB Sentral and CIQ border checkpoint. Affordable shopping, food courts, and money changers — a backpacker essential.

15 min by Grab

Arulmigu Sri Rajakaliamman Glass Temple

Culture & Heritage

The world's first and only Hindu glass temple, with over 300,000 pieces of glass adorning the walls, ceiling, and pillars. A breathtaking cultural landmark.

12 min by Grab

Danga Bay Waterfront

Leisure & Nature

Waterfront leisure park along the Straits of Johor with panoramic Singapore views. Night market, seafood restaurants, cycling paths, and a spectacular sunset spot.

10 min by Grab

Taman Pelangi Night Market

Food & Markets

Weekly night market right in our neighbourhood — satay, grilled corn, rojak, local kuih, and buzzing street food atmosphere. A true JB experience.

8 min walk

Sultan Abu Bakar State Mosque

Culture & Heritage

One of Malaysia's most stunning mosques, blending Victorian architecture with Moorish design. Overlooking the Straits of Johor with sweeping coastal views.

15 min by Grab

Forest City

Leisure & Nature

A futuristic coastal city featuring duty-free shopping, water theme parks, mangrove eco-tours, and beachfront dining. Great for a half-day trip from JB.

40 min by car

Legoland Malaysia

Theme Park

Asia's first Legoland theme park with over 70 rides, water park, and SEA LIFE aquarium. Perfect for families and big kids alike.

25 min by car

RTS Link & Singapore Day Trips

The Johor Bahru–Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS) Link is set to transform cross-border travel. Until it opens, the Causeway remains your fastest route.

RTS Link (Coming Late 2026)

Game-changer for cross-border travel

  • Route: Bukit Chagar (JB) ↔ Woodlands North (SG)
  • Journey time: ~5 minutes rail + immigration
  • Capacity: 10,000 passengers per hour per direction
  • Benefit: Skip the Causeway traffic entirely
  • From hostel: Bukit Chagar station ~10 min by Grab

Causeway (Available Now)

The classic JB-SG crossing

  • Bus CW1: JB Sentral ↔ Kranji MRT — RM 2.50
  • Grab: ~RM 40–60 one-way to Singapore city
  • Peak hours: 7–9am and 5–8pm weekdays
  • Pro tip: Travel before 7am or after 9pm for no queues
  • Full guide: Read our Causeway guide →
